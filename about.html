About the PFAC

Patient and Family Advisory Council at the N.C. Basnight Cancer Hospital

What we do

The Patient and Family Advisory Council (PFAC) partners with patients, families, and staff to improve the care experience. Members share perspectives on communication, education materials, workflows, and the overall patient journey.

Who participates

PFAC members include current or former patients and caregivers who want to help shape cancer care at UNC Lineberger.

Time commitment

Meetings and activities vary throughout the year. The PFAC team will discuss availability, interests, and opportunities during onboarding.
